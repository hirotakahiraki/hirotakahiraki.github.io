I am a **Research Scientist on the HCI team at [SB Intuitions](https://www.sbintuitions.co.jp/)**, which I joined in September 2026. That same month, I received my **Ph.D. in Interdisciplinary Information Studies** from the Graduate School of Interdisciplinary Information Studies at the University of Tokyo. My research in **Human–Computer Interaction (HCI)** focuses on wearable devices and speech interfaces that help people communicate when speaking aloud is difficult or their surroundings are noisy.

This work began with a question during my commute: could I make a phone call on a train without disturbing others? During my master’s studies, I developed mask-based interfaces that infer speech from mouth movements. I now work across **sensor design and machine learning**, from recognizing extremely quiet whispers with [SilentWhisper](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3612004) to capturing speech in noisy environments with [ClothTalk](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2025.3631752) and [MaskClip](https://doi.org/10.1145/3745900.3746088).

My goal is to **turn research into tools people can use in everyday life**. I bring together hardware, software, design, and prototyping, and value opportunities to share working prototypes with people beyond academia at Maker Faire and other exhibitions. Outside research, I enjoy ball juggling.

I have been an Individual Researcher in **JST ACT-X** since 2023, continuing into its Acceleration Phase in April 2026. In December 2025, I joined the **University of Washington**, Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering, as a Research Scholar (Internship).
