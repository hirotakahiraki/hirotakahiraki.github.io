I am a **Research Scientist on the HCI team at [SB Intuitions](https://www.sbintuitions.co.jp/)**. My research focuses on **human–computer interaction**, particularly voice interfaces, silent speech recognition, and wearable sensing. I work across sensing hardware and machine learning to make voice interaction practical in everyday environments.

I received my **Ph.D. in Interdisciplinary Information Studies from the University of Tokyo**, advised by **Jun Rekimoto and Yoshio Ishiguro**. Previously, I was a Research Assistant at **AIST’s Human Augmentation Research Center** and a Research Scholar (Internship) at the **University of Washington’s Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering (UW CSE)**, advised by [Shyam Gollakota](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~gshyam/). I have also been an Individual Researcher in **JST ACT-X** since 2023, continuing into its Acceleration Phase in 2026.
