I am a doctoral student at the **University of Tokyo**, advised by [Prof. Jun Rekimoto](https://lab.rekimoto.org/). My research is in **Human–Computer Interaction (HCI)**, with a focus on wearable devices and speech interfaces that extend human communication.

I build interfaces that let people communicate through **silent mouth movements, very quiet whispers, and speech in noisy environments**. My work connects sensing hardware, machine learning, and interaction design to make these possibilities part of everyday life.

I also work as a software engineer and enjoy ball juggling.
