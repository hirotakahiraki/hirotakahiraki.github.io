\vspace{-1.5em}

I am a Research Scientist on the HCI team at \href{https://www.sbintuitions.co.jp/en/}{SB Intuitions}, a SoftBank company focused on generative AI research and development. My research focuses on \textbf{speech interfaces and human-computer interaction}, particularly \textbf{speech input through very quiet utterances and in noisy environments}. I combine hardware prototyping, embedded systems, signal processing, and machine learning to develop speech interfaces for everyday use, including silent and whispered speech.
